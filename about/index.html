About James Portman

I'm a multidisciplinary creator working across screen, page, and canvas.

I design and ship full-stack web apps — increasingly with AI in the loop — make a microdrama series, write near-future fiction, and paint original landscapes of Georgian Bay and the Ontario region. My recent work spans a solo-built geography game, Baylight: After Image (a story told as microdrama, novel and audiobook, in five languages), and an online gallery of my paintings and photographs.

Whether the medium is code, film, prose, or oil, the throughline is the same: make the thing end to end, and sweat the craft.

Let's make something.
