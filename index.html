Terra Incognita

Baylight: After Image

A near-future surveillance thriller — “the city doesn't punish, it helps” — told across three forms: a 23-chapter novella, a full audiobook, and a microdrama series. The novels are available in English, Mandarin, Spanish, French and Portuguese. Episodes on YouTube with more in Production. The whole world lives on a custom, hand-coded static site I built as its hub.

Original Recording

A free prequel to Baylight: After Image, as a short story and a narrated audiobook of about eight minutes. Ava Chen spends three days in a studio recording 14,000 vocal fragments — syllables, breaths, the word please forty-two times, each with a different weight — and signs away perpetual rights to her vocal identity. Years later the city speaks to eight million people in her voice. They didn't steal a voice. They stole a way of caring. Available in English, Mandarin, Spanish, French and Portuguese.

2026 World Cup Pool

A solo-built, open-source bracket-pool app that ran through the 2026 FIFA World Cup (USA · Mexico · Canada). Players created a pool, shared a join code with friends, and the leaderboard updated as results came in. The tournament is over and the app is still up, /architecture page and all — it was built end to end in a few evenings.
